Federal Announces New Terminal Ascent Hunting Handgun Ammo

The three new handgun-hunting rounds feature a bonded, nickel-plated bullet design to increase weight retention, while a unique elastomer-filled nose cavity reliably expands.

 (Photo provided by Federal Premium.)

January 19, 2026

By Federal Press Release

ANOKA, Minnesota – January 19, 2026 – Federal Ammunition has announced its new Terminal Ascent Handgun ammunition line, built to deliver excellent accuracy and terminal performance for hunters who carry a handgun into the field. The three new handgun-hunting rounds feature a bonded, nickel-plated bullet design to increase weight retention, while a unique elastomer-filled nose cavity reliably expands. The new loads include 10mm Auto 203-grain, 357 Mag. 157-grain, and 44 Rem. Mag. 235-grain.
“Handgun hunters shouldn’t have to compromise when it comes to accuracy and terminal performance,” said Federal’s Handgun Ammunition Product Manager, Chris Laack. “Terminal Ascent Handgun delivers the trusted, high-performance Terminal Ascent design in powerful handgun chamberings, giving hunters confidence in the field.”
Terminal Ascent Handgun loads feature a bonded bullet design that locks the jacket to the lead core for increased weight retention, while the elastomer-filled hollow-point nose cavity is engineered to expand reliably on a wide range of game. For dependable performance in harsh hunting environments, each cartridge includes a nickel-plated case and bullet, a sealed primer to prevent corrosion, and specially selected propellants to ensure consistent ignition and accuracy in all conditions.
The new products will be displayed at the 2026 SHOT Show, January 20–23 at the Venetian Expo and Caesars Forum in Las Vegas, Nevada. Attendees of the show are encouraged to stop by Booth No. 11838 for a first-hand look at these new products and more.

Federal Ammunition products are available at dealers nationwide and online. For more information on all Federal products and to sign up for notifications about new product availability, rebate promotions, and other brand news via email, visit
