Taurus Introduces the All-New Brace-Ready TX22 Pistols

The newest TX22 models will feature the Strike Industries FSA Lite Brace and a threaded barrel.

 (Photo courtesy of Taurus)

October 15, 2026

By Taurus Press Release

Building on the proven performance, reliability, and value that have made the 
TX22
 a favorite among rimfire enthusiasts. The 
latest evolution of the platform
 introduces enhanced shootability, improved control, and refined ergonomics with the addition of the Strike Industries FSA Lite folding brace.
The TX22 has earned its reputation for delivering exceptional rimfire performance, combining dependable operation, outstanding ergonomics, and an enjoyable shooting experience. The Brace-Ready TX22 builds on that foundation, offering shooters a new level of stability and control while maintaining the characteristics that made the original TX22 so successful.
(Photo courtesy of Taurus)
The Strike Industries FSA Lite folding brace provides an additional point of support, helping shooters maintain greater stability and control. Its lightweight construction and compact folding design complement the TX22 platform, creating a more controlled and comfortable shooting experience without adding unnecessary bulk.
"The TX22 has set a high standard for what shooters should expect from a rimfire pistol, and we're always looking for ways to build on that success," said Bret Vorhees, President and CEO of Taurus USA. "The Brace-Ready TX22 represents another step forward for the platform, bringing together innovative design, improved shootability, and the dependable performance our customers have come to expect. It's a great example of how we continue to evolve our products while staying true to the Taurus commitment to quality."

(Photo courtesy of Taurus)
The Brace-Ready TX22 will come either equipped with the Strike Industries FSA Lite folding brace, or in a brace-ready configuration. The TORO Slide is optics-ready, cut for RMSc Pattern optics. Equipped with a threaded barrel and shipping with one 22-round magazine and one 16-round magazine. State-compliant models are also available, expanding access to the newest member of the TX22 family.
Consider Pairing the Brace-Ready TX22 with the all-new TTX22 titanium suppressor from Taurus USA for the ultimate rimfire combination, bringing together enhanced stability, superior control, and sound suppression in one exceptionally fun-to-shoot package.
