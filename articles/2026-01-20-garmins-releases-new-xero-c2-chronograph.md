Garmin's Releases New Xero C2 Chronograph

Compact and reliable ballistics chronograph adds rapid-fire tracking, Garmin smartwatch integration,
Applied Ballistics connectivity and more!

 (Photo provided by Garmin.)

January 20, 2026

By Garmin Press Release

OLATHE, Kan./Jan. 20, 2026/Outdoor Wire – Garmin (NYSE: GRMN) is expanding its advanced chronograph lineup with the introduction of Xero® C2. Building upon the success of its predecessor, 
Xero C2
 adds faster processing capabilities that allow for new features like rapid-fire tracking and resume session, plus Garmin smartwatch integration and Applied Ballistics® connectivity. The pocket-sized chronograph is easy to set up and remarkably accurate, measuring projectiles from 100 to 5,000 feet per second for bullets, arrows, slugs, airsoft BBs and more.
“Whether the target is a mile away or 40 yards, Xero C2 is the perfect companion for a day at the range. Combining performance and reliability, this chronograph lets marksmen truly focus on their shot,giving them confidence that they can track data with an accuracy they can trust.”
—Susan Lyman, Garmin Vice President of Consumer Sales and Marketing
What’s New
Rapid-fire support: Track up to 10 shots per second, depending on projectile velocity.
Applied Ballistics connectivity: Transfer muzzle velocity straight into the Applied Ballistics solver for long-range shooting solutions on a compatible smartphone.
Resume session: Switch between different firearms or test multiple bows or arrows and view data for each dedicated session.
Smartwatch Integration
When using a compatible Garmin smartwatch, like the new
 tactix® 8 – Cerakote® Edition
, marksmen can easily access session controls, view live chronograph data and import muzzle velocity into the Applied Ballistics ecosystem.

ShotView App
Use the ShotView™ app on a compatible smartphone to record results, compare velocity for every shot fired, view live sessions data, track deviation and more. The new configurations feature also lets users manage and track projectile and load data for firearms and bows. And during a shooting session, if more than one Xero C2 is paired to the ShotView app, marksmen can conveniently start a session on one chronograph and continue it on another.
Purpose-Built Design
Compact and easy to transport, Xero C2 features a rugged design and reinforced housing that withstands recoil when mounted to a firearm. It also has an IPX7 water rating and sunlight-readable, high-contrast display that is easy to see while at the range. And the rechargeable lithium-ion battery makes it possible for users to shoot up to 2,000 shots (or up to 6 hours) on a single charge.
Available to purchase on www.garmin.com starting January 23, 2026, Xero C2 has a suggested retail price of $699.99. See it on display at the Garmin booth, #10119, during SHOT Show 2026. To learn more, visit 
www.garmin.com
