2026 Guns & Ammo Holiday Gift Guide

The Holidays are fast approaching, so give the sportsmen and firearm enthusiasts the gift of Guns and Ammo!

November 02, 2026

By Guns & Ammo Staff

The holidays are a great opportunity to upgrade a shooting enthusiast's gear, add to the gun room, or find that one piece of equipment they haven't gotten ahold of yet. From innovative rifle accessories and precision shooting equipment to premium hearing protection, secure firearm storage and range-day essentials, this year's holiday gift guide features something for nearly every shooter. Whether you're shopping for a dedicated hunter, competitive shooter, AR enthusiast or recreational clay target shooter, these tools give practical performance that will make the season memorable.
Smith & Wesson M&P Shield X Carry-On Bundle
(Photo courtesy of Smith & Wesson)
MSRP
: $599
The Smith & Wesson 
M&P Shield X Carry-On Bundle
 makes an appealing gift for someone looking to add a versatile, optics-ready handgun to their collection. Built around the M&P Shield X platform, this package combines the company's slim, concealable pistol design with an included optic and complementary accessories. The bundle provides an opportunity to get started with an optics-equipped carry setup without having to shop around for accessories or an optic. For shooters interested in a modern everyday-carry platform, it's a holiday package backed up by a reliable industry legend.

For the hunter who appreciates versatile, lightweight equipment, the 
Spartan Precision Equipment MagnaSwitch system
 deserves a place on the holiday shopping list. Its magnetic attachment technology allows compatible bipods, tripods and shooting supports to be quickly attached or removed from a rifle, giving hunters the flexibility to carry their equipment separately until needed. The system also makes it easier to switch between compatible shooting supports depending on the terrain and shooting situation. Its practical advantages are explored in 
Guns & Ammo's review of the whole system
. Compatible mounting adapters and accessories are available through Spartan Precision Equipment.
Die Free Co. Rifle Furniture
(Photo by Michael Anschuetz)
MSRP
: Varies by product; the Kung Fu Grip starts at $20.
A rifle's ergonomics can make a significant difference in how comfortably and naturally it handles, and 
Die Free Co. (DFCO)
 offers a collection of accessories designed with that philosophy in mind. Best known for its iconic Kung Fu Grip, DFCO has expanded its lineup to include rifle furniture, hardware and other accessories that combine distinctive styling with practical functionality. The Kung Fu Grip features a 12-degree grip angle designed to promote a more natural wrist position, particularly on modern, compact rifle configurations. For shooters interested in personalizing their rifles, DFCO offers plenty of options, with new color options coming out for the Holiday Season. Our full review of the DFCO AR catalog 
is available online
.

Wiley X Shooting Eyewear
(Photo courtesy of Wiley X)
MSRP
: Varies by model and lens configuration.
Quality eye protection is an essential part of any shooting enthusiast's equipment, making 
Wiley X eyewear
 a much-needed holiday gift that doesn't sacrifice style for safety. The company's extensive lineup includes sunglasses, tactical eyewear and shooting-specific protective glasses designed to combine impact protection with visual clarity and all-day comfort. Multiple frame styles and lens configurations allow shooters to choose eyewear suited to their particular activities, whether spending a day at the range, hunting in changing light conditions or enjoying outdoor recreation. With options ranging from traditional shooting glasses to premium polarized sunglasses, Wiley X offers something for just about everyone on your holiday list.
B5 Systems Rifle Stocks
MSRP
: Starting at $61 for the Bravo Stock; varies by model.
With new changes to NFA enforcement, stocks are flying off the shelves as short barreled rifles grow in popularity. B5 Systems has earned a strong reputation for 
producing quality rifle furniture
, including stocks developed to meet demanding military and law enforcement requirements. Its commercial lineup brings that experience to civilian shooters, offering a variety of stock designs that emphasize durability, comfort and practical ergonomics. The popular Bravo Stock provides an ergonomic cheek weld, a cushioned non-slip buttpad, integrated sling attachment points and a compact profile. Other options, including the Enhanced SOPMOD and Collapsible Precision Stock, offer additional configurations for different rifle setups. Our staff 
can attest to the quality
 of B5 stocks.

Give the clay target shooter on your holiday list something a little different with the 
Caldwell ClayCopter Surface-to-Air launcher
. Designed to launch Caldwell's novel spinning ClayCopter targets, this portable system brings a new dimension to backyard clay shooting. Weighing less than 10 pounds, the freestanding launcher features a rechargeable 12-volt lithium-ion battery capable of powering up to 300 launches per charge. Shooters can adjust target presentations with oscillating and wobble functions, while wireless remote operation and Bluetooth connectivity through the Caldwell Clays app provide additional flexibility. The system can also launch singles or pairs and connect with compatible additional throwers. It's an great way to add variety and challenge to a day at the range, or setting up a feasable clay course on the property.
Silencer Central Gift Card
(Photo courtesy of Silencer Central)
MSRP
: Available in $50, $100, $250, $500, $750 and $1,000 denominations.
Much like with stocks, suppressors can't come out fast enough after the landmark NFA rulings. Gifting a whole suppressor is still not something recommended though, and shooter preference is a huge factor. Instead, a 
Silencer Central gift card
 is an easy way to let them choose something they'll genuinely appreciate. Available in several denominations, these electronic gift cards can be used toward eligible purchases through Silencer Central. Recipients can put the gift toward suppressor-related purchases and accessories, making this a particularly thoughtful option for the enthusiast who has been considering adding suppression to their collection. Gift cards are delivered electronically after purchase and can be printed, saved or forwarded.
Real Avid Ratchet Rest
(Photo by Alfredo Rico)
MSRP
: $44.99-$79.99, depending on configuration.
For the precision-minded shooter, or for the hunter that'd just grabbed a new scope, 
Real Avid's Ratchet Rest
 offers a clever upgrade over traditional shooting bags. Built around the company's Accu-Click technology, the adjustable-height shooting bags feature an integrated ratcheting mechanism that allows shooters to make precise elevation adjustments without constantly repositioning the rifle or stacking additional bags. A non-slip base, durable waxed-canvas construction and moisture-resistant bead fill provide a stable shooting platform. Available in large front-rest, medium front/rear-rest and small rear-rest configurations, the Ratchet Rest system is a useful addition to a rifle bench or range kit. 
Our review
 includes a full guide on how to sight in a rifle, taking full advantage of the Ratchet Rest.
Hornady VEXIS Safes
(Photo courtesy of Hornady)
MSRP
: Starting at $415.99 for the Night Guard; Mobilis models start at $3,173.99.
Hornady's new 
VEXIS security lineup
 brings an innovative approach to firearm storage with its patent-pending palm vein recognition technology. Unlike conventional fingerprint readers, VEXIS scans the unique vein pattern beneath the user's palm, providing contactless access in less than half a second. The lineup includes the Night Guard bedside safe, larger Mobilis modular safes and premium vault doors. The Mobilis models are particularly noteworthy for their modular construction, which allows the safes to be transported in sections and assembled in place. Additional features include heavy-duty steel construction, customizable Square-Lok interior organization and backup keypad and mechanical-key access. For a closer look at the company's modular safe design, Guns & Ammo 
recently evaluated the Mobilis Double Door
, and Hornady now offers VEXIS upgrade kits for older Mobilis models.
ShellShock EXO Hearing Protection
(Photo courtesy of Shell Shock)
MSRP
: $55-$119, depending on model.

Advertisement

Protecting your hearing is one of the most important considerations for anyone who spends time at the shooting range, and 
ShellShock offers several EXO hearing protection options
 worth considering. The lineup includes passive and electronic earmuffs, with features designed to balance hearing protection, comfort and situational awareness. The EXO Pro electronic model uses digital sound compression to suppress harmful noise while amplifying quieter ambient sounds, allowing shooters to hear conversations and range commands. Gel ear cushions provide additional comfort and help create a better seal around eyeglasses, while the low-profile earcups accommodate a more comfortable rifle cheek weld. For shooters who spend extended time at the range, the EXO Pro Elite adds upgraded sound amplification and additional convenience features. It's a practical gift that can help protect one of a shooter's most valuable assets.
PROOF Research Barrels and Rifles
(Photo by Michael Anschuetz)
MSRP
: Varies by product and configuration. PXT barrel blanks start at approximately $1,199, with pre-fit barrels starting at approximately $1,349.
For the serious rifle enthusiast, few upgrades are as meaningful as a quality barrel, and 
PROOF Research
 has established a reputation for precision-engineered barrels and complete rifle systems. Its lineup includes carbon-fiber-wrapped and traditional steel barrels, along with complete rifles designed for hunters and precision shooters. One of the company's latest innovations is Proof Exponential Twist (PXT) technology, which gradually increases the rifling twist rate along the barrel rather than immediately applying the full twist rate. The approach is designed to improve bullet engagement and consistency while potentially extending barrel life. The technology and its real-world performance received a closer examination in our full 
review of the Elevation MTR FDX Rifle
. For anyone looking to upgrade a favorite rifle or invest in a new precision platform, PROOF Research deserves consideration.
