## Article Text (extracted)

Smith & Wesson Introduces No-Lock Model 66 Combat Magnums

The new 4.25-inch and 2.75-inch models of these iconic revolvers are now available. 

 (Photo courtesy of Smith & Wesson)

November 24, 2026

By Smith & Wesson Press Release

Built on the iconic K-Frame platform, these new revolvers combine the proven performance 
of the Model 66 with enthusiast-preferred features,
 delivering a classic configuration that honors the legacy of one of Smith & Wesson’s most respected combat magnums.
The 
Model 66 Combat Magnum 2.75-inch
 is a compact, powerful revolver that is easy to carry without sacrificing capability. 
The 4.25-inch
 offers the same legendary K-frame handling in a versatile, duty-sized configuration. Chambered in .357 Magnum and .38 Special +P, both 6-shot revolvers feature stainless-steel barrels and fluted cylinders, textured black synthetic grips, and a serrated ramp front sight with red insert paired with a fully adjustable rear sight. Incorporating a yoke detent lockup system for enhanced cylinder lockup and alignment, along with a full-length extractor rod for positive case extraction, these new Model 66 Combat Magnums deliver dependable performance and offer enthusiasts a modern interpretation of a timeless classic.
