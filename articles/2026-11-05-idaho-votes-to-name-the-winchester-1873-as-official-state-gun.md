Idaho Votes to Name the Winchester 1873 as Official State Gun

"The Gun That Won The West" has been voted to become official firearm of The Gem State.

November 05, 2026

By Antonio Acitelli

Idaho voters have given a strong nod to one of America's most recognizable frontier firearms: the Winchester Model 1873 lever-action rifle. Idaho is now the 12th state to have an official firearm. Granted, this vote was a nonbinding advisory vote established through House Bill 932. The results recommend the Model 1873 to the Idaho Legislature, but lawmakers must pass separate legislation before it becomes Idaho's official state firearm. However, the chances of this decision becoming official are quite strong.
The November 3, 2026, vote offered voters the choice of six historically significant American firearms, representing everything from the Old West to the Second World War. 
Per the Idaho Secretary of State’s Office
, the choices (and vote totals) were:
Winchester Model 1873:
 164,405 votes
Winchester Model 1894:
 133,877 votes
Colt Single Action Army (Peacemaker): 
80,696 votes
Remington Model 700:
 65,826 votes
Colt M1911:
 46,407 votes
M1 Garand:
 37,506 votes
Two guns on the list have already been designated official state firearms; the Colt Single Action for Arizona, and the M1911 for Utah. The two Winchester rifles on the list dominated the poll with over 56% of the total vote.
The Model 1873 built upon Winchester's earlier lever-action designs, offering a practical repeating rifle at a time when many firearms still required individual loading. Originally chambered for Winchester's .44 Winchester Center Fire cartridge, the rifle helped popularize metallic-cartridge repeating firearms. The cartridge was also available for compatible revolvers, making it particularly useful for people who wanted a common ammunition supply.
