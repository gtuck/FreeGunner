Customizing Your AR with Die Free Co.

Aesthetics & ergonomics aren't exclusive to one another, and Die Free Co. is here to prove it.

 (Photo by Mark Fingar)

October 12, 2026

By Mark Fingar

Based in the “Live Free or Die” state of New Hampshire, Die Free Co., LLC (DFCO) is bridging the physiological gap between shooter and firearm. While best known for its popular Kung Fu Grip, DFCO has expanded into a full lineup of rifle accessories and magazine upgrades, each designed to complement modern shooting techniques and ergonomics.
Kung Fu Grip
(Photo by Mark Fingar)
Many shooters have used the ubiquitous AR-­15 pistol grip. The first was developed in the 1950s for bladed shooting stances for a longer length of pull. However, shooters today square up to a target and run compact rifles, tucking their elbows close to the body. Many shooters’ wrist are at an unnatural position with the A1/A2’s rearward cant of 25 degrees. 
The Kung Fu Grip
 solves this with a lesser 12-­degree grip angle to align the skeletal structure of the hand, wrist and forearm into a natural and more comfortable profile. According to DFCO, this alignment relieves tension on the ulnar nerve, helping our fine motor skills.
Designed as a replacement for any rifle with a standard AR-­15 footprint, it ships with all the necessary mounting hardware and installs in minutes. A deep finger groove on the front under the triggerguard provides excellent indexing. The “retro” texture on the sides and front keeps our hand from sliding out of position when operating the firearm.
An 
optional Plug
 snugs into the lower cavity and carries two CR123, AA or AAA batteries. It has a slot for a CR2032 battery, a cavity for a small hex wrench and a slot for a MultiTasker NANO Tool. The Plug also adds a quarter-­inch of length for those shooters with larger hands.

Tiger Claw 
(Photo by Mark Fingar)
The 
Tiger Claw
 is a compact, low-­profile, angled foregrip intended to replace straight vertical grips that create an unnatural wrist angle. The Tiger Claw features a 10-­degree forward angle, providing the correct shape for the support hand to wrap up and around the handguard to pull the rifle securely into the shoulder pocket. It aids greatly in stabilizing the rifle and achieving maximum accuracy. It securely attaches with two M-­Lok T-­nuts.
Tiger Claw XL
(Photo by Mark Fingar)
Evolving from the compact Tiger Claw, 
the XL variant
 is a full-­length grip that offers additional control surface. Like the Tiger Claw, it has a 10-­degree forward angle and is styled to complement the look and geometry of the Kung Fu Grip. The Tiger Claw XL allows shooters with loaded handguards the ability to easily control a firearm.
Full Stop
(Photo by Mark Fingar)
The 
Full Stop
 is M-­Lok compatible, machined from 6061-­T651 aluminum, and is designed as a hand stop for short handguards, or as a barricade rest when supporting the rifle against a wall or partition. The 1.3-­inch vertical front face has checkering to bite into support material, helping keep the rifle on target. When used in combination with a Tiger Claw, it creates a pocket that locks the user’s support hand in place.

Quick Stop
(Photo by Mark Fingar)
Essentially a shorter version of the Full Stop, the 
Quick Stop
 is a Melonite QPQ-­coated, stainless-steel accessory serves as both a micro barricade stop and quick-detach sling cup. The canted angle of the socket keeps a mounted QD sling from rotating and prevents it from twisting during transitions. The .40-­inch flat front also provides a low-­profile surface when steadying a rifle against barricades.
Dos Boot 
(Photo by Mark Fingar)
The 
Dos Boot baseplate
 upgrades a Magpul PMAG Gen M3 in three ways. First, it adds an additional two-­round capacity while easing spring pressure. It makes full-magazine reloads easier on a closed bolt.

Second, its extended profile gives the shooter more surface area to grab for faster magazine manipulation, even when stressed. Finally, the treaded footprint turns the magazine into a rigid monopod, delivering a stable platform when shooting prone or bracing against flat surfaces.
DFCO’s plastic products are available in black, Flat Dark Earth (FDE), Olive Drab (OD) green or Jungle Gray. Additionally, the Kung Fu Grip and Tiger Claw are available as stenciled Cerakote-­camouflaged sets in Alpine, MultiCam Black, “69” Tiger Stripe, Duck, M81 Woodland and MultiCam patterns. Every item in the DFCO lineup
is molded or machined in the United States.
