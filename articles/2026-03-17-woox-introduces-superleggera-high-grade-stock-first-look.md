WOOX Introduces Superleggera “High Grade” Stock: First Look

The new high grade lightweight hunting stocks use American Walnut and are designed for bolt action enthusiasts. It's a drop-in installation with no gunsmithing required. 

March 17, 2026

By WOOX Press Release

“Each piece is hand-selected and matched for its exceptional quality, grain depth, and contrast. We look for wood that stands apart from the rest and put them together to form a matching pair. The result is a truly unique stock set where no two sets are the same. This High-Grade match is beauty in the most natural form and gives our end user a collector-grade build,” said Ben Fleming, CEO of WOOX USA. “With limited supply of this High Grade American Walnut, we’re going to build every one of them we can. This beautiful Walnut deserves to be shown off, and we know our customers will appreciate the unique and rare quality that this series brings.”
The new Superleggera “High Grade” line represents a significant expansion of WOOX’s stock lines, expanding its signature Italian-American design to a growing audience of bolt-action enthusiasts. The Superleggera stock is a great lightweight option for unmatched durability and accuracy. 
Weighing in at just 33 to 35 ounces, the Superleggera represents a great option for premium wood stocks. The WOOX Superleggera stock is a bolt action with a classic look. This high performance stock delivers exceptional accuracy and reliability for demanding shooters. With an adjustable length of pull from 13.5” to 14.5” (spacers also included) the chassis can fit a wide range of rifles, making it the perfect stock for any situation. The stock fits: CZ 457, Howa 1500, Remington 700, Ruger 10/22, Tikka T3, Savage 110 and Ruger American models. To see all options 
click here
.
In recent tests, the Superleggera produced group sizes down to .278 MOA, which is a drastic improvement over the factory stock accuracy. With design features like an adjustable cheek riser, a vertically adjustable recoil pad and its lightweight construction, shooters have all the features they need to increase accuracy. It is a true drop-in install that does not require pillars or extra bedding.
