July 30, 2026

By Beretta

Accokeek, MD
(July 28, 2026)
 – Beretta USA is excited to announce the launch of the 
A400 Solo
, a new semi-automatic shotgun engineered for hunters who demand uncompromising reliability, speed and performance in the harshest conditions. Built on Beretta's proven A400 platform and packed with the company's latest advancements, the A400 Solo delivers faster cycling, softer recoil and exceptional ballistic performance in a rugged, purpose-built hunting shotgun.
The A400 Solo is engineered for hunters who demand uncompromising reliability, speed and performance in the harshest conditions. (Beretta photo)
Designed to thrive where conditions are at their toughest, the A400 Solo combines Beretta's innovative 
B-LINK
®
 Pro gas-operating system
, 
Steelium
®
 barrel technology
 and 
Kick-Off
®
 Plus recoil reduction system
 into one dependable platform. Whether chasing waterfowl through flooded timber or walking upland fields, the A400 Solo is built to keep hunters focused on the shot, not their equipment.
"Our goal with the A400 Solo was to build a shotgun that hunters can rely on in any environment," said Caleb McMillen, Beretta USA Sr. Product Manager for Long Guns. "Every feature was designed to help hunters perform with confidence, from faster cycling and reduced recoil to easier maintenance and legendary Beretta barrel performance. The A400 Solo is built for those who refuse to let the elements dictate their hunt."
The B-LINK Pro Gas System is Beretta's fastest, cleanest and most reliable gas-operating system to date. (Beretta photo)
