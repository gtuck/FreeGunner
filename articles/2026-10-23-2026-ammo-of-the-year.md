2026 Ammo of the Year

6.5 Creedmoor +Peak Alloy

 (Photo by Michael Anschuetz)

October 23, 2026

By Guns & Ammo Staff

Federal Premium’s Peak Alloy case technology was recognized for its part in the 
7mm Backcountry cartridge winning Guns & Ammo’s 2025 Ammo of the Year award.
 What we didn’t expect was the integration of Peak Alloy case technology with such a popular cartridge: 6.5 Creedmoor.
The only path to a higher-­velocity version of the 6.5 Creedmoor was for Federal to ensure it was safe and compatible with rifles already on the market. The characteristics of Peak Alloy case technology under pressure made it possible. Riflemen now have the option of shooting cartridges factory loaded to 80,000 pounds per square inch (psi) instead of the usual 50,000 to 60,000 psi.
Peak Alloy is a monolithic steel-­alloy case that allows high-­pressure shooting. The brass in brass-­cased cartridges starts to flow at about 65,000 psi, which spikes the pressure on a bolt face. Peak Alloy is a combination of metals that increase strength. The internal volume of the case is the same and neck tension in factory loaded ammo is consistent, resulting in exceptional accuracy.
Federal selected the 6.5 Creedmoor cartridge for its first application of +Peak Alloy after surveying the big-­game hunting market. The decision ensured that Peak Alloy case technology could be experienced by as many rifleshooters as possible without requiring them to purchase a new rifle. It also allowed Federal’s manufacturing team to easily transition from making the brass-­cased version to steel-­alloy cased loads.
