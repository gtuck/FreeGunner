New Smith & Wesson Handguns at SHOT Show

Pistols include new Spec series handguns and the return of Night Guard Revolvers.

January 20, 2026

By Smith & Wesson Press Release

At SHOT Show 2026, Smith & Wesson have announced the release of the new S&W Spec Series R 686 Plus and Spec Series VI M&P9 M2.0 Metal Compact.
Spec Series – Model 686 Plus
Introducing the first-ever revolver from the Spec Series line: The Specs Series R. This model perfectly blends classic aesthetics with modern enhancements. Built on the robust Model 686 L-Frame, this 7-shot revolver is chambered in .357 Magnum/.38 Special +P and weighs 42.4 ounces. Its 4.13-inch stainless-steel barrel features a Power-Port™ at the end, reducing felt recoil for quicker target acquisition. Out of the box, it comes equipped with an extended Performance Center cylinder release, an Aimpoint ACRO P-2 Red Dot Reflex Sight mounted on a custom base for an exceptional sight picture, and both a red ramp front sight and adjustable rear sight. The Spec Series R frame features a durable Tungsten-color Cerakote finish that finely contrasts with its black PVD fluted cylinder and traditional checkered wood grips. Additional items included with the purchase are a Savior Equipment pistol bag, a Spec Series R knife, a challenge coin, patch, and 10 compatible moon clips.
Spec Series VI – M&P9 M2.0 Metal Compact
The Specs Series VI boasts a feature-rich design that delivers an unparalleled performance package within a compact carry platform. This new M&P pistol, chambered in 9mm Luger, is constructed on a 7075 T-6 aluminum frame, showcases a distinct champagne Cerakote finish, and has a capacity of 15+1. The in-line porting at the 12 o’clock position of its 4-inch barrel efficiently expels gas in intervals, significantly reducing muzzle rise, which allows users to reacquire their target more quickly. At the base of the grip, a Floyds Custom Shop EDC magwell promotes a higher grip for improved control during firing and also facilitates more efficient reloads. For compatibility, the three included 15-round magazines also feature Floyds Custom Shop EDC baseplates. To complete this ultimate concealed carry kit, this pistol includes an Aimpoint ACRO P-2 Red Dot Reflex Sight, which directly mounts to the slide from the factory, a Hunter Constantine Carry Belt designed for all-day comfort and exceptional support, and a Savior Equipment Pistol Bag. Customary to Spec Series models, this bundle also ships with a Spec Series VI knife, a challenge coin, and a patch.
Lipsey's Exclusive Model 386 and Model 396 Night Guards
Originally launched in 2008, the Night Guard Series consisted of compact, double-action revolvers designed for personal defense, concealed carry, and duty use. From 2008 to 2012, eight production models were available across K, L, and N-Frames, with caliber options ranging from .38 Special, to .45 ACP.

Coming soon, a pair of new production Night Guard revolvers will be available to purchase through a licensed dealer near you. The 7-shot Model 386 chambered in .357 Magnum/ .38 Special +P and the 5-shot Model 396 in .44 Special. Both models are engineered with a no-lock scandium frame and 3-inch barrel to deliver the ideal lightweight profile. Built with Smith & Wesson’s upgraded Endurance Package, these revolvers offer enhanced durability and smooth performance shot after shot. Out of the box, they ship with an XS Sights tritium dot front sight and fully adjustable rear sight to ensure accuracy, and also include a Hogue Rubber Bantam grip that provides superior control, balance, and comfort.
Whether for personal defense or professional use, the new Night Guard Series combines a proven design with modern enhancements—giving you a revolver that’s ready when it matters most. For more information, visit 
www.smith-wesson.com
