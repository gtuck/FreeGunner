Springfield Armory XD Mod.4 OSP: First Look

The newest generation of the XD pistol is here.

March 24, 2026

By Springfield Armory Press Release

The 
XD Mod.4 OSP
 (which stands for “Optical Sight Pistol”) features a factory-milled slide that employs a plate-based system to accommodate today’s most popular optics, enhancing accuracy and performance. OSP plates for the pistol are available for purchase in the 
Springfield Armory Store.
The pistol’s aggressively textured polymer frame ensures superior control, with the texturing strategically placed on the most important contact points. Additionally, an undercut trigger guard and extended beavertail allow for a high-hand position for superior control. Matching up with the frame are enhanced slide serrations — located both fore and aft — that are deeply cut and aggressive, providing enhanced grip and confident manipulation even in adverse conditions.
In addition to its great handling characteristics, the XD Mod.4 OSP also features a full set of safety features. The passive grip safety adds a layer of safety and is disengaged with a proper shooting grip. The striker’s position is confirmed by a status indicator located on the rear of the slide. And lastly, a tactile loaded chamber indicator slightly protrudes from the top of the slide when a round is chambered.
The 9mm pistol features a flat-faced trigger to provide a consistent feel throughout the trigger press. In addition, a red fiber optic front sight is paired with a white dot rear, both constructed of steel for durability. A MIL-STD-1913 Picatinny rail is located on the dustcover portion of the frame. The XD Mod.4 ships with a 16-round stainless steel magazine, and the MSRP is $399. A 10-round variant for restricted locales is also available.
