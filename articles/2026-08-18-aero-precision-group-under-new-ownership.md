Aero Precision Group Under New Ownership

Aero Precision, Stag Arms, Ballistic Advantage & VG6 are looking to come back strong.

August 18, 2026

By Aero Precision Press Release

Aero Precision, Ballistic Advantage, Stag Arms, and VG6
 are transitioning from private equity ownership to a family investment group. White Wolf Capital is no longer involved with the businesses in any way. The new ownership group is made up of successful, self-made businesspeople with experience building and operating businesses. They are not currently part of the firearms industry, but they are firearms and outdoor enthusiasts, strong supporters of the Second Amendment, and strong supporters of our military and veteran communities.
The family investment group has chosen to remain private. We respect that decision and ask others to do the same. The firearms businesses are owned and operated independently under Aero Ballistic Holdings LLC. Aero Precision, Ballistic Advantage, Stag Arms, and VG6 will all continue as distinct brands and remain a core part of the business moving forward.
The new owners believe strongly in what is already here: established brands, proven products, talented employees, loyal customers, and significant American manufacturing capabilities. The intent is not to reinvent the companies, but to get them back to doing what they do best. Manufacturing is ramping back up, supply chains are being restored, and the teams are working to increase product availability and fulfill the significant backlog of existing orders and commitments. Customers and partners should expect to see product availability improve as operations come back to strength, along with the return of core products and new product launches.
The past few years have been difficult for the companies, their employees, customers, dealers, distributors, vendors, and partners. There is still work ahead, and rebuilding trust will take time. But the brands made it through an incredibly challenging period because a lot of people continued to believe in them and stand behind them. We would not have made it to this point without that support, and we are incredibly grateful for it.
