Remington Ammunition Offers Three New Subsonic Rifle Loads in 2026

The new Boat-tail Hollow Point (BHP) loads include 360 Buckhammer 250-grain, 308 Win. 190-grain, and 45-70 Govt. 300-grain.

 (Photo provided by Remington.)

January 16, 2026

By Remington Press Release

LONOKE, Ark. – January 15, 2026 – Remington Ammunition will launch three new rifle cartridges loaded to subsonic muzzle velocities in 2026. The new Boat-tail Hollow Point (BHP) loads include 360 Buckhammer 250-grain, 308 Win. 190-grain, and 45-70 Govt. 300-grain.

The new centerfire rifle load offerings will expand upon Remington’s existing rifle, handgun, and rimfire Subsonic loads, and are engineered for optimal performance when paired with suppressors. They are loaded with custom powders and dependable primers. These loads' performance and design prevent supersonic cracks, while the enclosed base bullets reduce lead buildup in suppressor ports.

“Remington Subsonic outperforms competitors superbly, without excessive noise. The product line is built for shooters who want quiet, consistent performance without sacrificing reliability,” said Remington’s Centerfire Rifle Product Director Mike Holm. “These new rifle loads expand the lineup with more options for hunters, training enthusiasts, and tactical shooters who need dependable accuracy below the speed of sound.”

Each Remington Subsonic load is assembled with tight quality control and premier components to ensure consistent subsonic performance. Built for versatility, these loads provide quiet operation, reduced recoil, and minimal shooter signature, making it an adaptable choice for hunting, training, and tactical applications across a wide range of environments.

Attendees of the 2026 SHOT Show, January 20–23 at the Venetian Expo and Caesars Forum in Las Vegas, Nevada, are encouraged to stop by Booth No. 11838 for a first-hand look at these new products and more.

Remington Ammunition products are available at dealers nationwide and online. For more information on Remington ammunition and to sign up for product news and special offers, visit
