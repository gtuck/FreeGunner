BANISH HD 762 INC Suppressor: First Look

The all-inconel 7.62 suppressor pairs low back pressure with maximum flash and sound suppression for AR-10s, 6mm AR-15s and short-barreled rifles.

 (Photo courtesy of BANISH)

November 06, 2026

By BANISH Press Release

Building on the proven design of the BANISH HD 556 INC, 
the HD 762 INC
 is the second suppressor in BANISH’s Heavy Duty (HD) line, reserved for suppressors built for hard use and heavy fire rates. It is engineered to take the abuse of AR-10s and 6mm AR-15s. Nine baffles and eight helical vents route expanding gas along a controlled path, keeping back pressure low on gas-operated platforms without giving up suppression or flash reduction.
“Gas guns are hard on a suppressor, and too much back pressure makes them hard on the shooter, too,” said Kelly Streiff, BANISH Suppressors chief revenue officer. “The HD 762 INC is built from Inconel to handle that punishment, and the controlled-flow design keeps the rifle running clean and comfortable. We expect it to become the go-to suppressor for anyone running an AR-10, a gas-operated precision rifle or an SBR.”
The HD 762 INC measures 6.7 inches long and 1.65 inches in diameter and weighs 15.9 ounces without a mount. It is full-auto rated, carries no barrel-length restrictions and handles cartridges from 5.56 NATO up to .300 RUM, including 6.5 Creedmoor and other high-pressure cartridges.
Built to perform with both supersonic and subsonic .30-caliber cartridges, the HD 762 INC averaged 135.7 decibels at the shooter’s ear in internal testing with supersonic .308 Winchester ammunition through a 16-inch AR-10. With subsonic .300 Blackout ammunition through a 16-inch AR, that average drops to 126.2 dB.

Each HD 762 INC ships with a 5/8x24 direct-thread mount for simple installation on the rifle of the owner’s choice. The suppressor also uses industry-standard HUB threads, making it compatible with all major quick-detach mounting systems, while 1.9 inches of space to the first baffle leaves room for full-length muzzle brakes and flash hiders.
Available in Black and Flat Dark Earth Cerakote finishes, the HD 762 INC is made in the United States and backed by the BANISH lifetime warranty, which protects against all manufacturing defects and damage caused by normal use. Owners can also use the BANISH Service Program for a comprehensive maintenance check within a year of ownership. The HD 762 INC has an MSRP of $1,129.
The BANISH HD 762 INC is available now through BANISH Suppressors dealers. To learn more about the HD 762 INC or to shop the brand’s full offering, visit 
BANISHsuppressors.com
.
