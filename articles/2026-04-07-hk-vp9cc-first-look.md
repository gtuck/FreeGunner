HK VP9CC: First Look

New for 2026 is the VP9CC, a no-compromise, fully featured micro compact 9mm handgun from HK.

 (Photo by Michael Anschuetz)

April 07, 2026

By Heckler & Koch Press Release

Manufactured in HK’s Oberndorf factory and imported for the U.S. market by HK-USA, the VP9CC sets a new standard for versatility, ergonomics, and reliability in the concealed carry category. Every VP9CC comes fully equipped with features typically reserved for larger pistols. From its customizable ergonomics to its optics-ready configuration, the VP9CC delivers a solid shooting experience in a compact form factor.
The VP9CC is built to meet or exceed NATO AC/225 standards, ensuring durability and reliability under demanding conditions. It features HK’s cold hammer-forged, cannon-grade steel barrel and the same advanced hostile environment coating found on HK’s full-size German-made pistols. Despite its compact size, the VP9CC offers impressive capacity with 10+1 and 12+1 magazine options.
The pistol includes six interchangeable backstraps—Small, Medium, Large, and extended variants of each—allowing users to achieve a truly custom fit. A crisp, striker-fired trigger with an approximate 5-pound pull provides a predictable break and clean reset, enhancing accuracy and control. The grip features a refined texture pattern, with smoother sidewalls for all-day carry comfort and aggressive texturing on the front and rear for secure handling.
(Photo by Michael Anschuetz)
The VP9CC is optics-ready out of the box, featuring a proprietary deep slide cut that lowers the optics mounting height by 40% compared to traditional designs. This enables true co-witness capability with the factory-installed sights, which include a tritium front sight and a serrated, blacked-out rear sight for reduced glare. Each pistol ships with an RMSc mounting plate, while additional plates, including RMRcc compatibility, are available through the HK webshop. The optics-equipped package includes a factory-installed Vortex Defender CCW enclosed emitter optic for enhanced performance and durability.

The VP9CC maintains the fully ambidextrous controls that define the VP9 family. Features include HK’s paddle-style magazine release, ambidextrous slide catch and release, and the brand’s signature charging supports for improved slide manipulation.
Additional features include a Picatinny rail for accessories, a visual and tactile loaded chamber indicator, and tool-free disassembly for easy maintenance. The pistol also includes multiple redundant safety mechanisms, including trigger safety, firing pin/drop safety, out-of-battery safety, and a safe takedown feature, ensuring confident handling in all conditions.
The VP9CC is available in two configurations: An optic-ready model for $1,049, or a version with a pre-installed Vortex Defender CCW for $1,399. With the VP9CC, HK delivers a combination of precision engineering, superior ergonomics, and cutting-edge features into a platform designed for modern concealed carry. For more information, visit
