**Original Link:** [Smith & Wesson Expands M&P15 Line with New AXE Pistols and SBRs in .300 Blackout](https://www.gunsandammo.com/editorial/mp-axe-sbr-300-blackout/556008)

## Article Text (extracted)

Smith & Wesson Expands M&P15 Line with New AXE Pistols and SBRs in .300 Blackout

The newest addition to the AXE platform was built with suppressed shooting in mind.

 (Photo courtesy of Smith & Wesson)

July 21, 2026

By Smith & Wesson Press Release

Designed with suppressed shooting in mind, the 
M&P15 AXE Pistol
 leverages Gemtech’s GVAC direct impingement gas system. This purpose-built technology streamlines gas flow through the platform, helping reduce excess gas exposure and delivering a more premium shooting experience to the user.
Controls for both he pistol and SBR versions of the M&P15 AXE are ambidextrous. The SBR variant includes folding sights and an adjustable stock. (Photo courtesy of Smith & Wesson)
Chambered in .300 Blackout, the M&P15 AXE Pistol ships with a 30-round magazine and features an Armornite-finished 8-inch 5R-rifled barrel with a 1:7.5-inch twist rate. Other key features include a 5/8x24 threaded barrel, a 7-inch Midwest Industries Combat M-LOK free-float handguard, and an enhanced bolt carrier group. The pistol also utilizes a Radian Raptor ambidextrous charging handle, is equipped with ambidextrous controls, features an M&P pistol grip, and comes fitted with a Magpul BTR arm brace. 
The SBR variant of the AXE
 uses an adjustable Magpul CTR stock, as well as folding sights.
(Photo courtesy of Smith & Wesson)
Built on the proven M&P15 platform, the M&P15 AXE Pistol is designed for reliable performance across a wide range of applications and is backed by Smith & Wesson’s Limited Lifetime Service Policy.
