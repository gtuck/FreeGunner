The Best SIG Sauer P365 Pistols with Full Reviews

Which of the 20+ model variants of the P365 best fit your needs? 

March 11, 2026

By Guns & Ammo Staff

Is there a best SIG Sauer P365 pistol? The gun has long evolved from its original model introduced in 2018, 
earning Guns & Ammo’s coveted Handgun of the Year award
. It’s more accurate to say it is now a platform, and the line has evolved with the everyday carry (EDC) and conceal carry trends. There are at least 20 different models of the P365, though many are simply different in color.
The big four options to choose from are the SIG Sauer P365-XMACRO, P365-Fuse, P365-AXG Legion, and the P365 Rose series. This roundup has complete reviews of each of the big four, but there are numerous options within each line, including models with compensators, grip size, barrel length, optic ready and much more. Additionally, SIG Sauer has an extensive line of optics, lights and other accessories optimized for the P365, many of which are also reviewed in this roundup.
The SIG Sauer P365 is well established as a quality EDC pistol, and it maintains the trendsetter status it immediately achieved with its launch. The question is which model is the best? That answer is obviously subjective, as each brings unique benefits for self-defense. Guns & Ammo has put the primary P365 models through in-depth testing and evaluation. Read on to see if one fits your needs.
SIG Sauer P365-FUSE
The original P365 is iconically compact in size. The P365-Fuse goes the other direction, boasting an impressive 17-round magazine capacity as well as an extended 21-round capacity. It is a full-size pistol, though it maintains the P365 design influence. Despite its size, Handguns Editor James Tarr finds it to be perfectly capable as a daily carry, even concealed. It requires a good holster and extra considerations its compact parent doesn’t need to worry about, but its grip length makes it far more shootable, especially beyond 10 yards. Tarr goes on to explain the Fuse benefits over a SIG Sauer P320, and 
offers his full review here
.

SIG Sauer Rose
The days of suggesting a snubby .38 Special or a colorful semiautomatic .22LR for women are over. The SIG Sauer Rose is more than a gun designed for women, the series also incorporates training and community for women to better and more comfortably carry a firearm. Donna Boddington is as passionate a shooter and hunter as her husband, Craig Boddington. She performs an in-depth review of two Rose P365 pistols and was impressed with their performance. More than that, though, Donna Boddington examined the training and community SIG Sauer has built around the Rose program. The Rose P365 is for women serious about everyday carry. 
Check out her complete review here
.
SIG Sauer P365-AXG Legion
“I carry a full-­size handgun because my philosophy is this: If all I’ve got is a handgun, I want it to be one that can solve any problem a handgun is capable of solving. This means good sights, controllability, and sufficient power and capacity to keep me on the right side of the dirt. The P365-AXG Macro offers all of this and more in a gun that’s more concealable than a full-­size gun,” writes James Tarr. His final summary of the AXG Legion speaks volumes. Blending compact and full-size features, the Legion pistol is an all-metal construction, offering superior durability and reduced recoil with the added weight. 
You can read Tarr’s complete review here
.
SIG Sauer P365-XMACRO
The SIG Sauer P365-XMACRO is unique within this roundup because it ships with an integral compensator. At 3.1 inches, the barrel is relatively short, but the compensator adds more length to the slide. Reviewed by Jeremy Stafford, he found that the comp helps reduce recoil with so-called “hot” 9mm loads, but it does come at a cost of increased concussion while shooting. Ultimately, Stafford found the comp to be effective and well-integrated. In line with newer P365 models, the XMACRO is optic ready and includes a rail for lights and other accessories. Stafford especially like the sandpaper-like grip texture while shooting. Capacity is excellent at 17 rounds, and 
you can review his full range report here.

SIG Sauer P365 California
Unfortunately, some lawful citizens have to deal with unlawful regulations, especially in states like California. As legal battles continue, pistols like the SIG Sauer P365 California are a good option until such a time as rights are restored. This review of the P365 California dives into the specific regulations the SIG Sauer had to build around to offer a P365 to California residents. Though limited to 10-round magazines, 
the P365 California
 offers almost all of the same features as the other models in this roundup. Built with the same quality and durability, the P365 is an excellent home defense and EDC pistol for restricted states.
Sharps Bros P365 Grip Module
With the popularity of the P365, an extensive aftermarket has inevitably popped up around the platform. One such example is the Sharps Bros P365 grip module. Since the P365 operates on the SIG Sauer Fire Control Unit (FCU), which is the only serialized part, it’s easy to swap the FCU with other grip frames and slides. These parts can be shipped to your front door, too. 
Reviewed by Alfredo Rico
, he found the wood grip panes of the frame to be attractive and comfortable in the hand. Rico found it easy to swap install the FCU into the Sharps Bros frame, and you can read about the entire process and evaluation here.
