UTG PRO Introduces New MP5 SD Monolithic Handguards

Modern MP5 Modifications

 The monolithic design solves for both optics and accessories attachments. (Leapers photo)

November 03, 2026

By Shooting Times Staff

Made for the modern MP5® enthusiasts. The monolithic design solves for both the optics and accessories attachments. It provides ample real estate for a variety of accessories and optics. This single product would typically take two to three products to achieve the same result. Available in MP5® and MP5K options, and as a complete set or forearm add-on only.
Improved ergonomics cater to different-sized shooters, providing more locations to effectively hold, place, and use accessories, with maximum real estate for placing optics, even for those who are restricted in eye relief and exit pupil.
The increased 2.1” inner diameter prevents accessories mounts conflicts and helps with heat dissipation to keeping the handguard comfortable to the touch. Encasing the suppressor increases safety, making it unlikely to burn someone or their gear. Plus, the design looks super slick.
Patented, Highly Configurable Designs
The patented monolithic design, first introduced for the standard MP5 handguards, incorporates three separate patents that work to provide ample real estate for a variety of accessories and optics. The new SD versions are launching in MP5® and MP5K options as a complete set or forearm add-on to retrofit existing monolithic handguards.

The SD design offers maximum flexibility with dozens of M-LOK ® slots and more than a dozen Picatinny tops. This single product would typically take two to three products to achieve the same result. Beyond accessory versatility, the extended handguard design offers more gripping surface and improved ergonomics for shooters of varying sizes. Whether prioritizing comfort, control, or a personalized setup, users gain more options without the complexity of multiple mounting components, even for those who are restricted in eye relief and exit pupil.
Designed with Suppressor Integration in Mind
The expanded 2.1" interior diameter helps reduce potential mounting interference while allowing greater airflow around the suppressor, keeping it cool to the touch. The surrounding handguard also provides a physical barrier that can help reduce incidental contact with hot suppressor surfaces and nearby equipment. The result is a functional, streamlined design that combines practical benefits with the sleek, integrated appearance modern MP5 enthusiasts appreciate.
