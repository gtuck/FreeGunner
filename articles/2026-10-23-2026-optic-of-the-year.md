2026 Optic of the Year

Nightforce NX6 Series

 (Photo by Michael Anschuetz)

October 23, 2026

By Guns & Ammo Staff

Competitive pricing meets lightweight optical performance. 
The NX6 series
 incorporates the same experience and technology that made the Nightforce brand a legend on the battlefield and in competition. The NX6 scopes make Nightforce more affordable and easier to use afield.
There are many notable features on the NX6 series. For example, lenses are bedded and the shooter can change turrets for the ideal setup on a particular rifle, and then change the configuration when mounting the scope to a different firearm.
Zeroing is easy and the eye relief is comfortable. Elevation and windage turrets are capped for the NX6 1-­6x24mm low-­power variable optic (LPVO) offering an illuminated reticle featuring an off position between intensity settings.
(Photo by Michael Anschuetz)
The NX6 lineup is large, including the 1-­6X LPVO, 2-­12x42mm, 3-­18x50mm, 4-­24x50mm, 5-­30x56mm and 6-­36x56mm. For most power ranges there is a first focal plane (F1) and a second focal plane (F2) version. (The exceptions are currently the 4-­24X and 5-­30X.) Each configuration is also offered with a couple of milradian (mil) or minute-­of-­angle (MOA) reticle options optimized for the power range. The FC-­MRx is a new 20 MRAD (64 MOA) grid reticle with Digillum technology to enhance low-­light engagements. You’ll find the FC-­MRx in the 2-­12X while the MOAR and 4Ai are reticles in the 3-­18X and 4-­24X scopes.

The 1-­6X and 2-­12X are great options for short-­range opportunities, especially for hunting game in the north and eastern U.S. These provide quick target acquisition. The 3-­18X models are all-­purpose scopes while the 4-­24X was developed for western and predator hunters. The 5-­30X scopes are offered with the MOA-­C, MOA-­XT or MIL-­C or MIL-­XT for confident long-­range shooting across a wide range of magnification. For precision at distant targets, look at the 6-­36X. Varmint hunters will want the 6-­36X with the FVR-­1 reticle.
The NX6 series is a Nightforce product for the masses at a lower pricepoint. Here’s to clarity for all.
