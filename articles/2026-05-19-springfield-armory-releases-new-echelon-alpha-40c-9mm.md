## Article Text (extracted)

Springfield Armory Releases New Echelon Alpha 4.0C 9mm

A Price-Optimized Echelon Pistol

 The grip module retains all the features that make this a great shooting pistol. (Springfield Armory photo)

May 19, 2026

By Shooting Times Staff

Is the Springfield Armory Echelon a little out of your budget? You’re in luck, Springfield Armory just introduced the 
Echelon Alpha 4.0C 9mm
 with an MSRP of $599. That’s $111 less than the Echelon 4.0 C.
For California residents, a California-Compliant Echelon Alpha 4.0C 9mm is available for a slightly higher MSRP of $649. The reason for the uptick in price is that California requires new semi-automatic pistol models to go through special laboratory tests. The manufacturer pays for the testing, registration, and annual maintenance fees.
If you're living in a restrictive state, an Echelon Alpha Low Capacity model is available. It includes a 10-round magazine instead of a 15-round magazine. MSRP is $599.
Minor differences like a polymer optics plate and simple white dot front sight reduce production costs without sacrificing performance. (Springfield Armory photo)
Differences at a Glance
The Echelon Alpha retains many of the same features as the 
Echelon 4.0 C
. The differences include a polymer optics plate, recontoured slide, and a white-dot front sight in place of a tritium front sight with luminescent ring. Instead of two stainless-steel 15-round magazines, only one is shipped with the pistol. Also, you’ll find a medium interchangeable backstrap; the small and large sizes are sold separately.

Standard Features
At the heart of the Echelon Alpha is the same stainless steel Central Operating Group serialized chassis. This allows the modular grip to be easily removed and paired with different grip modules and slides.
The Variable Interface System accommodates over 30 optics directly to the slide without the use of adapter plates.
The Echelon Alpha retains the same forward-thinking features, such as a hammer-forged barrel, an ambidextrous slide stop and magazine release, an oversized trigger guard, the Adaptive Grip texture, and textured indexing points.
