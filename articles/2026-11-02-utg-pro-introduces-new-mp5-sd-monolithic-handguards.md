UTG Pro Introduces New MP5 SD Monolithic Handguards

UTG Pro's new SD Handguards are in stock and shipping to dealers.

November 02, 2026

By Leapers Press Release

The patented monolithic design, first introduced for the standard MP5 handguards, incorporates three separate patents that work to provide ample real estate for a variety of accessories and optics. The 
new SD versions are launching in MP5 and MP5K
 options as a complete set or forearm add-on to retrofit existing monolithic handguards.
The SD design offers maximum flexibility with dozens of M-LOK slots and more than a dozen Picatinny tops. This single product would typically take two to three products to achieve the same result. Beyond accessory versatility, the extended handguard design offers more gripping surface and improved ergonomics for shooters of varying sizes. Whether prioritizing comfort, control, or a personalized setup, users gain more options without the complexity of multiple mounting components, even for those who are restricted in eye relief and exit pupil.
Designed with Suppressor Integration in Mind
The expanded 2.1" interior diameter helps reduce potential mounting interference while allowing greater airflow around the suppressor, keeping it cool to the touch. The surrounding handguard also provides a physical barrier that can help reduce incidental contact with hot suppressor surfaces and nearby equipment. The result is a functional, streamlined design that combines practical benefits with the sleek, integrated appearance modern MP5 enthusiasts appreciate.
Availability and Ordering
The new UTG PRO SD Monolithic M-LOK Handguards are launching in limited quantities, with dealer shipments beginning now. The SD handguards join a growing family of UTG PRO accessories designed for MP5 platforms, including: the Extended Cocking Handle released this past spring, the patented Light Mount, and the UTG Pro Claw Mount, Extended Picatinny.
