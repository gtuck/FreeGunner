WOOX Introduces New Bravado “Grey Laminate” Stocks

Adding strength and style to your lever-action.

April 29, 2026

By Guns & Ammo Staff

WOOX, have announced the introduction of the 
new Bravado “Grey Laminate” line of stocks
. These new stocks are precisely engineered for compatibility with Henry and Marlin lever-action rifles.
“The Bravado Laminate represents a bold expression of WOOX craftsmanship, where advanced materials converge with refined design,” said Ben Fleming, CEO of WOOX USA. “Built from precision-layered hardwood laminates, this laminate version features distinctive grey and black striations that create a contemporary and dynamic appearance that is sure to please the most discerning lever-action aficionados.”
The Bravado Laminate’s layered construction generates contrasts and patterns that defines its character through controlled engineering and careful material selection. To preserve and emphasize the laminate’s visual identity, every Bravado in laminate is finished with a durable clear coat that seals the surface, enhancing contrast and definition while providing a smooth, uniform feel. The Bravado Laminate was designed to stand apart; the grey/black laminate gives it an unmistakable and visually stunning appearance.
The new Bravado “Grey Laminate” line represents a significant expansion of WOOX’s commitment to craftsmanship, expanding its signature Italian-American design to a growing audience of lever-action enthusiasts. Known for its blend of classic aesthetics and modern performance, the Bravado stock is a natural fit for lever-action platforms. The design provides enhanced stability, improved handling, and a uniquely elevated shooting experience, whether on the range or in the field.

The Bravado Grey Laminate stock’s modular design is compatible with both straight and pistol grip configurations. It features an adjustable cheek riser with 5 positions of adjustment for personal comfort. It’s designed with flared front and rear ends for improved control while the ultralight recoil pad minimizes recoil for a smooth shooting experience. Checkering ensures a secure hold, even in the most challenging conditions. QD sling mounts offer reliable mounting on both sides of the stock for convenient carry options. M-LOK compatibility allows for seamless accessory customization of both the stock and handguard.
The Bravado Grey Laminate stock is compatible with 120+ Henry rifle SKUs, including X Model, SPD HUSH, Big Boy, Large Frame, Lever Shotgun, and Golden Boy. It’s also compatible for all Marlin rifle SKU’s including the Dark series, SBL series, Guide Gun series, Trapper series and Classic series. Only the Bravado stock (not the handguard) is compatible with Classic series model 336/1894 and Guide Gun series model 1894.
Bravado Grey Laminate Stock & Handguard Kit MSRP: $549
Bravado Grey Laminate Stock Only MSRP: $329
Bravado Grey Laminate Handguard MSRP: $225
Like every WOOX creation, the “Grey Laminate” line of stocks are crafted in Italy, assembled and tested in the USA, and are built to last a lifetime. For more information about all WOOX premium stocks, knives, axes and accessories, please visit www.wooxstore.com and follow them on Facebook and Instagram.
