S&W Model 66 Combat Magnum: First Look

Smith & Wesson Brands, Inc. announces two new Model 66 Combat Magnum revolvers with a No-Lock frame

 The Model 66 Combat Magnum with a No-Lock frame.

November 25, 2026

By Shooting Times Staff

Smith & Wesson Brands, Inc. announces two new revolvers: the Model 66 Combat Magnum with a No-Lock frame. Built on the iconic K-Frame platform, these new revolvers build on the Model 66’s classic configuration.
Offered in 2.75-inch and 4.25-inch barrel lengths
Chambered in .357 Magnum and .38 Special +P
6-shot revolvers
Stainless-steel barrels
Fluted cylinders
Textured black synthetic grips
Serrated ramp front sight with red insert paired with a fully adjustable rear sight
Yoke detent lockup system for enhanced cylinder lockup and alignment
Full-length extractor rod for positive case extraction
For more information, visit 
www.smith-wesson.com
.
