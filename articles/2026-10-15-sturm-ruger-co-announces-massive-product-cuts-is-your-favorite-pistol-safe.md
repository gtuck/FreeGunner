The Sturm, Ruger & Co. lineup is undergoing a massive shakeup
 as an internal memo sent to distributors on 
October 13, 2026
, reveals sweeping changes under the company's new "Ruger 2030" strategy.
The leaked letter details a major product line rationalization that includes the immediate halt of flagship wheelguns like the GP100 and a planned phase-out of the SR1911 by the end of 2026. The move signals a hard shift away from low-margin or labor-intensive legacy platforms to make room for modernization, automation, and higher-demand products.
Here is a comprehensive breakdown of the affected models, the timelines outlined in the letter, and what it means for the future of Ruger firearms.
The Corporate Shift: "Ruger 2030"
According to the letter, the changes are driven by an ongoing review aimed at aligning production capacity with current market demand and manufacturing efficiency.

This creates future opportunities to bring legacy platforms back down the line using updated designs and more efficient, automated manufacturing processes.

The Takeaway for Buyers
If you have been eyeing a Ruger GP100, an SR1911, or a Precision Rifle, the clock is officially ticking. While Ruger intends to eventually re-engineer some of these lines for modern factories, it could be years before they return to store shelves. Expect current distributor inventories of these affected models to dry up rapidly as collectors and shooters rush to secure these legacy iterations.
