SIG Sauer Reveals the New P385 9mm

Revealed at SIG NEXT, the P385 is built from the ground up with a new fire control unit, and is compatible with P320 magazines.

 Clockwise from top right: The new P385 Mach30 Coyote, P385-Full, P385 MACH30 with SIG Lock Pro ROMEO-X red dot, and the P385 COMP.

September 22, 2026

By SIG Sauer Press Release

Unveiled at the premier 
SIG NEXT event
, the 
SIG SAUER P385
 delivers a dramatically reduced felt recoil for a softer, flatter, and faster shooting experience and provides maximum modularity with its innovative new fire control unit (FCU).
“SIG NEXT brings together many of our top partners at one of our premier events of the year,” said Robby Johnson, Executive Vice President and Chief Marketing Officer, SIG SAUER. “It’s an opportunity to not only showcase our latest innovation in firearms and electro-optics but also our latest technologies in R&D, Engineering, and Advanced Manufacturing, and provide a behind-the-scenes look into SIG SAUER.”
The P385
The P385 Compact model.
Built with performance at its core, the P385 features the new SIG Recoil Dampening System, a low bore axis, and a precision-cut carbon steel barrel for extreme accuracy. As a result, muzzle rise is reduced by 20 percent or more, depending on the specific P385 model. Along with its new FCU, the P385 includes a new SIG SAUER Trigger Block for a smooth, clean trigger press and pairs with the new MagStrap grip module for easy customization both in grip length and hand size.  
“The all-new SIG SAUER P385 was developed from the ground up,” said Eric McKenzie, Director of Commercial Product Management, SIG SAUER. “We’re driven to innovate, and the P385 delivers a new era of performance, accuracy, and innovation with several new features.”

Available in black and coyote brown, all models of the new striker-fired pistol are SIG-LOC optic-ready, feature fully ambidextrous controls, and have an optional manual thumb safety. The P385 will be available in stores this fall and early winter, with the lineup including the P385-MACH3D, P385-COMP, P385-FULL, P385-CARRY, and P385-COMPACT.
