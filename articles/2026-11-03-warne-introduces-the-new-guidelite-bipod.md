Warne Introduces the New GuideLite Bipod 

The GuideLite Bipod is engineered from the ground up to meet the demanding requirements of backcountry hunters.

 (Photo courtesy of Warne)

November 03, 2026

By Warne Press Release

Combining lightweight carbon fiber construction with rapid leg deployment, versatile positioning and a quick-detach mounting system, the 
GuideLite Bipod
 delivers the stability and adjustability hunters need in the field without unnecessary weight.
Starting at just 12.9 ounces, the GuideLite Bipod is available in 2 heights featuring extendable carbon fiber legs with an adjustable height range of 8 to 14 inches, or 11 to 22 inches, providing versatile support across a variety of hunting environments. Its intuitive leg design allows for rapid deployment from the horizontal position on one side, with additional positions at 0, 45 and 90 degrees on the other side to accommodate changing terrain and shooting positions.
(Photo courtesy of Warne)
The GuideLite Bipod features a quick-detach (QD) head with a Picatinny-style attachment for quick and easy installation and removal. It offers 60° of pan and cant, controlled by an indexable tension lever for enhanced adjustability in the field. A pan lockout function allows shooters to disable panning for a cant-only bipod configuration when a fixed shooting position is preferred.
Built for hunters who cover demanding terrain and prioritize efficient, lightweight gear, the Warne GuideLite Bipod combines practical functionality with the durability and performance expected from Warne products.
