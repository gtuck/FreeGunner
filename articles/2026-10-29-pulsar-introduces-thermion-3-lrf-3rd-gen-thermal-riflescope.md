Pulsar Introduces Thermion 3 LRF: 3rd-Gen Thermal Riflescope

Longer range, sharper image detail, improved handling and expanded functionality build on the familiar Thermion platform

 The third generation of its classic-design thermal riflescope is available in XR60 and XT60 configurations.

October 29, 2026

By Shooting Times Staff

(MANSFIELD, TEXAS) – Pulsar has introduced the Thermion 3 LRF, the third generation of its classic-design thermal riflescope, available in XR60 and XT60 configurations. Developed through refinement and customer feedback, Thermion 3 improves imaging performance, rangefinding, ergonomics and battery life while preserving the familiar handling of a traditional riflescope.
The Thermion series combined thermal imaging with the appearance and controls of a conventional daytime scope. The third generation carries the tagline “Perfected,” reflecting Pulsar’s focus on refining proven technology through practical field experience.
Key Improvements in Thermion 3
Extended laser rangefinding. The integrated rangefinder now measures up to 1,312 yards (1,200 meters), versus approximately 875 yards (800 meters) in the previous generation, and delivers faster readings.
Sharper thermal imaging. Updated sensors improve detail. The XT60 uses a 1280 × 1024 HD sensor; the XR60 features a next-generation 640 × 480 sensor.
Refined traditional controls. Zoom and focus rings retain the feel of a daytime scope, with a zoom throw lever and convenient top-turret focusing wheel.
Improved viewing comfort. A larger screen is intended to provide increased eye relief and reduce eye strain. Both models feature a 2560 × 2560 AMOLED display.
Longer operation. Improved battery life and a user-replaceable battery support extended use in the field.
Ballistic assistance. The integrated ballistic calculator and Stream Vision Ballistics compatibility support distance-informed shooting decisions.
Two Models for Different Hunting Applications
Thermion 3 LRF XT60. The flagship model pairs a 1280 × 1024 HD sensor with 2–24× magnification. Its 14.6° field of view delivers fine image detail and broad situational awareness for close- and long-range observation.
Thermion 3 LRF XR60. A next-generation 640 × 480 sensor and 4–40× magnification, including 4× base magnification, make this model particularly suited to open-field and longer-distance observation.

Both models feature a 60 mm (2.36-inch) F1.0 objective lens and a high-resolution 2560 × 2560 AMOLED display for image clarity and contrast.
Advanced Technology, Familiar Design
Thermion 3 retains a traditional riflescope profile and a standard 30 mm (1.18-inch) mounting tube for compatible conventional rings. The integrated laser rangefinder fits discreetly into the objective housing, avoiding a bulky external attachment. Ergonomic controls and a top-mounted focusing wheel further preserve the conventional scope experience.
Recording, Connectivity and Field Durability
Recoil-activated video recording and 128 GB of internal storage allow hunters to capture footage without separate recording equipment. Stream Vision 2 compatibility lets users manage footage and device settings from a smartphone or tablet, while Stream Vision Ballistics supports ballistic calculations.

An IP67-rated aluminum alloy housing protects the device against dust and temporary immersion under the rating’s specified conditions. Thermion 3 is manufactured in Europe and backed by Pulsar’s five-year warranty.
Built on Experience
From the extended rangefinder and improved sensors to refined controls and greater viewing comfort, Thermion 3 responds to the practical needs of hunters while retaining the familiar Thermion format. Pulsar is supporting the launch with a documentary following a professional hunter who supplies wild game to Michelin-starred restaurants.
The Thermion 3 LRF XR60 and XT60 will be available through Pulsar’s authorized dealer network. Product information and dealer locations are available at 
www.pulsarnv.com
