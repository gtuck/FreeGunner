Hodgdon Lil' Gun .357 Magnum Load Data

A Versatile Powder for Magnum Pistols

 Hodgdon Lil' Gun is a great choice for the .357 Magnum. 

September 08, 2026

By Joel J. Hutchcroft

From Ask the Experts
Q
. This question could be for Lane Pearce or Allan Jones or probably other Shooting Times writers. I think the burn rate of Lil’ Gun is close to that of H110 and W296, so can I use Lil’ Gun to load .357 Magnum handloads for a rifle and a handgun?
Jack DeYoung
, Via email
A
. You are correct to assume that Lane Pearce, Allan Jones, and other ST writers could answer this one, but I can handle it myself, so here’s my answer. 
Hodgdon’s online Reloading Data Center
 lists a few handloads for Lil’ Gun in the .357 Magnum for handguns but not for rifles. I exported that information and created the accompanying chart for your convenience. I encourage you to go online to the Data Center yourself, just to be thorough.
Joel J. Hutchcroft
, Shooting Times Editor
